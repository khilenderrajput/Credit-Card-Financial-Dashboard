# 💼 Credit Card Financial Analytics: Business Problem & Context

## 1. Executive Background

Consumer credit card portfolios are among the most revenue-generative yet risk-sensitive assets held by retail financial institutions. The health of a credit card business depends on balancing:
1. **Top-line revenue generation** (annual card membership fees, merchant interchange fees on transaction volume, and revolving interest charges).
2. **Customer acquisition & engagement velocity** (recouping marketing costs and driving immediate 30-day card activation).
3. **Credit risk & delinquency mitigation** (identifying early warning signs of non-performing accounts before charge-offs occur).

Without a unified analytics platform, financial executives and portfolio managers face fragmented data silos between core banking transaction logs and customer demographic databases.

---

## 2. Core Business Problems & Analytical Inquiries

This project was developed to answer key strategic questions across four core dimensions:

### A. Portfolio Revenue & Fee Optimization
- What is the total revenue generated by the portfolio, and what is the relative contribution of transaction volume vs. interest charges vs. annual fees?
- How is revenue distributed across card tiers (**Blue, Silver, Gold, Platinum**)?
- Is there an over-reliance on standard entry-level products (Blue card) at the expense of premium tiered interchange and fees?

### B. Transaction Spend Dynamics & Channel Behavior
- In which merchant categories are cardholders spending the most (**Bills, Entertainment, Fuel, Grocery, Food, Travel**)?
- What payment channels do cardholders prefer (**Swipe, Chip, Online**)?
- Are digital/online payment methods being under-utilized relative to physical point-of-sale swipes?

### C. Customer Demographic & Occupational Segmentation
- Which demographic cohorts represent the highest lifetime value (LTV) and gross spend?
- How do income brackets (**Low <$35K, Medium $35K-$70K, High >$70K**) and age groups correlate with credit limit utilization and total revenue?
- How do occupations (e.g., **Businessmen vs. White-collar vs. Govt employees**) differ in risk and profitability?

### D. Credit Risk, Delinquency & Acquisition Health
- What proportion of newly acquired card accounts activate within the critical first 30 days?
- What is the baseline delinquency rate across the portfolio, and which customer profiles present higher default risk?
- Are customer acquisition costs (CAC) aligned with product tier profitability, and what is the portfolio return on marketing investment?

## 4. Analytical Objectives & Success Metrics

1. **Consolidate & Normalize Data:** Unify disparate transaction and customer demographic tables, resolving date formatting discrepancies and categorical inconsistencies.
2. **Quantify Financial KPIs:** Establish an auditable data pipeline calculating **Total Revenue ($56.52M)**, **Transaction Volume (667K transactions / $45.53M spend)**, and **Interest Earned ($7.98M)**.
3. **Multi-Platform Enablement:** Provide analytical workflows across three tiers:
   - **SQL Analytics:** Robust query repository for data warehouse reporting and ad-hoc slicing.
   - **Power BI Architecture:** Enterprise data model, DAX measures, and dashboard blueprints for internal BI consumers.
   - **Interactive Web App (React + Vite):** A responsive, client-side executive dashboard deployed on Render for interactive stakeholder exploration.
